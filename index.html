Project terms

Clear scope. No surprises.

Please review the key terms before confirming a package.

Payment schedule
  • 40% advance payment upon project confirmation.
  • 40% when the working website is presented for review.
  • 20% before production deployment and handover.
Scope and revisions

Only the features listed in the selected package are included. Two revision rounds are included within the agreed scope. Unlisted functionality requires written agreement and may cost extra.

Client responsibilities

The client provides the logo, approved tour details, prices, descriptions and photographs, unless otherwise agreed. The client must review and approve tour information and pricing before publication.

Domain ownership and renewals

The developer will manage domain and hosting operations. The domain will be registered for the client's business, with registration details and ownership arrangements documented. First-year domain and hosting are included; renewals after year one are paid separately by the client.

Defect support

30 days of defect support after deployment covers reproducible faults in the delivered functionality. It does not cover new features, changed requirements, unauthorized modifications, or third-party service failures.

Third-party services and taxes

Paid third-party subscriptions, transaction fees and other external service costs are excluded unless stated otherwise. Any applicable taxes will be clarified before project confirmation.

Delivery and validity

Estimated delivery is 2–3 weeks for Basic, 3–5 weeks for Business, and 4–6 weeks for Premium, once scope, advance payment and required content are received. Proposal validity: 14 days.

Bookings and online payments

Automated booking management, online payment processing, customer accounts, reservation workflows and complex seasonal pricing are excluded unless separately agreed in writing.
